FleetTrack — Fleet Management System

FleetTrack is a fleet management web application developed using Django and PostgreSQL. It supports essential fleet operations, including vehicle and driver management, trips, expenses, maintenance, fuel records, alerts, and vehicle documents.

Key Features

  • Fleet dashboard and vehicle management
  • Driver and trip management
  • Fuel, expenses, and maintenance records
  • Alerts and vehicle document management
  • User authentication and administration
